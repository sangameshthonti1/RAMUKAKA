## Honest presentation notes

- Say **"documentation simulation"**, not "live integration". The only real operations are local HTTP/UI/database actions.
- Root portal choice, header role selection and selected provider record are not authentication; a provider fixture or newly added provider is not a contracted or verified partner.
- Saved chat is deterministic and local, not live AI or a delivered messaging service. Household-entered asset dates do not establish warranty or AMC coverage. A scripted approval is not permission to move money, and no real money can move in this build.
- No market size, customer count, conversion, revenue, cost savings, partnerships or measured outcomes are asserted without evidence.
- Visual fidelity could not be established because the source directory was empty and reference site returned 401.
- This submission demonstrates a working local architecture and safety boundaries; production identity, service-proof intake, external integrations and operations remain future work.
